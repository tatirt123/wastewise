    Climate Action Tips

    Use a Reusable Bottle

    • Reduce plastic waste by using a reusable bottle instead of single-use plastic bottles.

    Plant Trees

    • Trees help absorb carbon dioxide and provide cleaner air for our communities.

    Save Water

    • Turn off the tap when it is not needed and avoid wasting clean water.

    Recycle Properly

    • Separate recyclable materials from biodegradable and residual waste before disposal.

    Use Reusable Bags

    • Reduce plastic waste by using reusable bags instead of single-use plastic bags.

    Today's Eco Challenge

    Avoid using single-use plastics today!
